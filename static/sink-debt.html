The TabPFN 130-Meal Dataset

The classification target divides human exhaustion into three strict regimes:

  • Zero Effort (Mug): 1 dish, 2.0 to 4.0 minutes active standing. Sloth Approval Score: 90–99.
  • Low Effort (1 Pan): 2 dishes, 4.5 to 8.0 minutes active standing. Sloth Approval Score: 65–85.
  • High Effort (Avoid): 4+ dishes, >12.0 minutes standing. Sloth Approval Score: <45.
